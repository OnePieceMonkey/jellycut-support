Questions.

Support
How do I restore my purchase?

Open the full-version screen in JellyCut and tap “Restore Purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. It works the same way on your other iPhones and iPads signed in with that account.

How does the free play work?

You get three minutes of active play. The clock only runs while you are touching the fruit — not while the app sits open in the background. After that, a one-time purchase lets you keep playing without limits.

Does reinstalling reset the free play?

No. The time you have used is stored in your device’s keychain, so it survives reinstalling. It never leaves your device.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, and so are refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment and cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

Does JellyCut collect my data?

No account, no ads, no tracking. Anonymous crash reports and the purchase check are the only things that leave your device. Details are in the privacy policy.
